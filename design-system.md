# Design system — Commonwealth's Attorney, 17th Judicial Circuit (Campbell County)

> Produced in-session from the client's seal and the three reference sites, using
> the `frontend-design` skill and the website-builder schema. "Claude Design" is
> not callable from this environment, so this file was not generated by it.

## Brand

- **Client:** Office of the Commonwealth's Attorney, 17th Judicial Circuit
  (Campbell County, Kentucky). Rob Sanders, Special Commonwealth's Attorney.
- **Voice / feel:** Authoritative, direct, public-facing. The confidence of a
  bold law-firm site with the restraint of a public office: big type, strong
  contrast and clear next steps, but nothing that sells. Plain language. The
  office speaks as "the office" or "we"; nothing is put in Rob's mouth unless
  the client supplies it.
- **Brand cues taken from the seal:** the seal itself, its royal navy field, its
  silver ring and lettering, the bronze of the frontiersman's coat, and its
  concentric double rings and all-caps gothic lettering.
- **Deliberately different from the Kenton County site** (separate office,
  separate identity): different typefaces, a different navy, and bronze in
  place of maroon.

## Typography

- **Heading font:** Besley (variable, 400 to 900; used at 600 and 700). A
  Clarendon revival: the sturdy bracketed slab of American courthouses, law-book
  spines and park signs. `font-heading`.
- **Body / UI font:** Libre Franklin (variable; used at 400, 500, 600, 700). An
  American gothic in the Franklin Gothic line, which also echoes the seal's
  lettering when set in tracked capitals. `font-sans`.
- **Eyebrow / label:** Libre Franklin 700, 12.5px, uppercase,
  `tracking-[0.16em]`. This is the seal's lettering, flattened.

## Spacing & layout

- **Base unit:** 4px (Tailwind default scale). Rhythm steps used: 8, 12, 16, 24,
  32, 48, 64, 96, 128.
- **Container:** `max-w-[76rem]` (1216px), centered, with 24px gutters on mobile
  and 40px from `lg`. Text-only blocks narrow to `max-w-[42rem]`.
- **Grid:** 12 columns from `lg`, 32px gaps. Asymmetric splits (7/5, 5/7) are
  preferred over even halves.
- **Section vertical rhythm:** 72px mobile, 96px tablet, 128px desktop
  (`py-18 md:py-24 lg:py-32`). Compact bands (utility strip, stat rows) use 40px.
- **Section tone alternates:** navy-900 hero → silver-50 → white → navy-900 →
  silver-100 → white → navy-950 footer. No two tinted sections in a row.

## Components

- **Buttons** (shadcn `Button`, restyled). Rectangular, 2px radius, 48px tall
  (`lg` is 56px), uppercase tracked label.
  - Primary on light: `navy-800` fill, white text; hover `navy-900`.
  - Primary on dark (the main CTA): `bronze-500` fill, `navy-950` text; hover
    `bronze-400`.
  - Secondary: transparent, 2px border (`navy-800` on light, `silver-300` on
    dark); hover fills with the border color and inverts the text.
  - Ghost / text link: label plus arrow; arrow moves 4px right on hover.
  - Focus: 3px `royal-600` ring with 2px offset on light, `bronze-400` on dark.
    Always visible on keyboard focus.
- **Cards** (shadcn `Card`, restyled). White, 1px `silver-200` border, 2px
  radius, no resting shadow. A 3px top rule (`navy-800`) grows from the left to
  full width on hover or focus. On dark sections: `white/5` fill, `white/15`
  border, bronze top rule.
- **Nav.** Three tiers, top to bottom: a `navy-950` utility bar (circuit and
  county on the left, emergency notice on the right) that scrolls away; a white
  header with the seal, a two-line wordmark, the primary links and a "Contact"
  button; a 3px bronze rule beneath. The header is sticky and gains a shadow
  once the page scrolls. The links show from 1280px. Below that they collapse
  into a shadcn `Sheet` opened from a menu button, and the "Contact" button
  stays in the bar from 768px.
- **Footer.** `navy-950`. Seal and office name, address, site links, then a
  hairline and a legal row for the policy links and "Cookie settings".
- **Form fields** (for later pages). 48px tall, 2px radius, 1px `silver-300`
  border, `navy-800` 2px border on focus, label above in Libre Franklin 600 14px.
- **Section headings.** A bronze label, the title, then an optional lead. No
  decorative rule or divider sits above the heading.
- **Page header** (inner pages). A `navy-900` band with the rings running off
  the right edge: a bronze label (the office name, or a link back to the parent
  page), the page title and an optional lead. Compact: 56px, 64px and 80px of
  vertical padding at mobile, tablet and desktop.
- **Office contact.** Another office's name, address, phone and hours on
  `silver-50` with a 3px bronze rule down the left edge. Used wherever the site
  sends people elsewhere.

## Imagery

- **Style direction:** the seal is the hero image. No stock photography. Where
  photographs are supplied later (a portrait of the Commonwealth's Attorney, the
  courthouse, Newport), they are documentary and unfiltered, set in squared
  frames with a 1px `silver-300` border.
- **Signature motif, "the rings":** concentric hairline circles in silver at low
  opacity on navy, radiating from the seal and running off the edge of the
  section. Used in the hero and once more, faintly, in the footer.
- **Iconography:** Lucide line icons, 1.5px stroke, 24px, in `navy-800` or
  `bronze-400` on dark. Used sparingly and never as decoration alone.
- **Corner radius:** 2px everywhere (`--radius: 0.125rem`). The only circles are
  the seal and its rings.
- **Shadow:** none at rest. A single soft shadow
  (`0 12px 32px -12px rgb(3 14 48 / 0.25)`) for the sticky header and raised
  hover states.
- **Borders:** 1px hairlines; 3px for accent rules.

## Motion

- **Page load (hero only):** eyebrow, headline, lead and buttons rise 12px and
  fade in, staggered 70ms apart; the rings ease out from 96% to 100% scale; the
  seal fades in last. Total under one second.
- **Scroll:** sections do not animate in. Content is never hidden waiting for a
  scroll trigger.
- **Hover / focus:** card top rules grow, link arrows nudge, buttons change fill.
  150 to 200ms, `ease-out`.
- **Reduced motion:** every animation and transition above is disabled under
  `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
